Common questions.

The things clients usually ask before we start.

  • Q01

    What does a typical engagement look like?

    A 1-week discovery sprint (goals, scope, fixed quote), then 2-week iterations with a live staging URL from day three, a Friday demo and a written changelog. You see progress weekly, not at the end.

  • Q02

    How do you price projects?

    Fixed price for well-defined scopes, weekly retainers for evolving products. Every quote is itemized by module, so you can trim scope without renegotiating everything.

  • Q03

    Can you take over an existing Laravel codebase?

    Yes — it's most of my work. I start with an architecture audit and a risk register, add test coverage around critical paths, then refactor incrementally. No big-bang rewrites.

  • Q04

    Do you provide post-launch support?

    Every build includes 30 days of free fixes. After that, maintenance plans cover security patches, dependency upgrades, monitoring and a guaranteed response SLA.

  • Q05

    How do you handle AI costs and hallucinations?

    Token budgets and caching are designed in from the start, every LLM feature ships with an eval suite, and high-stakes outputs get guardrails — structured outputs, retrieval grounding and human-in-the-loop review where it matters.

  • Q06

    Who owns the code?

    You do — 100%. Full repository access from the first commit, infrastructure in your accounts, and a handover document your next engineer will actually enjoy reading.
